# 07 — Safety, respectful culture and privacy (SPAWN-native V4)

## Religious and folk areas
Chính điện is quiet and dignified; no meme quẻ banner, no noisy compatibility ranking, no fictional “giải hạn” promoted as Buddhist liturgy. All fortune/love/xăm/cắt duyên âm/sao fictional mini-games are visually located in separate Z_FOLK or Z_LOVE. Display: “Trải nghiệm văn hóa, mang tính giải trí; không dự đoán hay thay đổi số phận.” Only verified, appropriately licensed text can be labeled Buddhist sutra. Do not create ghost diagnosis, harmful predictions, treatment claims or selling removal of curses/merit.

## Spawn account and safeguarding (owner decision)
Do NOT create an extra 18+ age gate or collect a birth date. Do NOT create "Khách xxxx" or cookie guest identity, separate friend/chat/DM/report service, or new login. Inherit **Spawn's existing** account/session, account protections, block/report/mute, and privacy policies; confirm coverage by two-session tests.

**Unverified youth safety = blocker for the affected social features, not automatic permission to enable them.** If Spawn has no suitable controls, disable stranger romance invites and unmoderated access rather than introducing an unapproved age gate. Pure temple exploration and solo practices can remain.

## Folk-fun social privacy
Opt-in default OFF; symbol SEN/CHUONG/TRA is a game-only preference for 5 minutes, expires automatically. Only consenting, genuine, unblocked nearby players in same room may be privately suggested; no avatar/birth date/real-life religion or content-derived matching. One player cannot broadcast another's name or “hợp tuổi/tình duyên”; no public pairing announcement without separately obtained consent of both. Share rare solo quẻ only after owner initiates preview and confirms; rate-limit to avoid social pressure. Quẻ results fiction, not real medical/legal/financial advice.

## Prayer/wishes and pictures
Personal wishes are private by default; until moderation exists, only curated preapproved templates may be shown to other players. No public health or contact info. Photo invites require consent and respect Spawn's capture/privacy controls; photo API is PENDING if undocumented.

## Data & security
Game stores only necessary opaque Spawn user reference, optional chosen outfit, private journal/cards, game interaction receipts and explicit sharing/matching preference if Spawn persistence supports it. No birthday, true name, GPS, microphones, phone/email from mini-games. Do not invent retention times; align with actual Spawn policies, EU GDPR obligations, deletion/export, moderation/legal review. No tracking SDK/real-money gambling/paid reroll.

Spawn must enforce block state and abuse rate limits on all game-side events where supported. No unsanitized player text in announcement templates. If privacy/safety capabilities cannot be verified in runtime, mark PENDING and block the feature instead of silently allowing unsafe behavior.

## Audio and assets
Ancient scripture copyright, modern translation rights, recording and TTS license are separate checks. Link official listening material but never copy recordings without permission. Author/license models and sound; fictional NPCs must not impersonate real temples/monks.
